# Fince News — Crypto & Finance Blogging Platform

A premier financial journalism and crypto blogging website designed in an elegant **White & Orange** theme, featuring a main home portal and 9 dedicated, comprehensive blog pages covering the latest developments in **Cryptocurrency**, **Global Finance**, **Fintech**, and **Macroeconomics**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Main Home Page (`index.html`)**:
   - **Live Financial Ticker**: Displays live pricing tickers for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, S&P 500, Gold, and Treasury yields.
   - **Hero Spotlight Article**: Featured hero card highlighting the top story.
   - **Instant Category Filtering**: Filter between *All Articles*, *Crypto & Web3*, *Global Finance*, and *Fintech & AI*.
   - **Live Real-Time Search**: Instant search filtering as you type across titles and excerpts.
   - **Full Blog Grid**: Displays all 9 blog articles with high-resolution imagery, category badges, author avatars, reading times, and snippets.
   - **Interactive Newsletter Signup**: Functional subscription form with instant feedback toast alerts.
   - **Dark / Light Mode**: Seamless theme switcher with persistent local storage preferences.

2. **9 Dedicated Blog Detail Pages (`blogs/`)**:
   - `blogs/1-bitcoin-milestones.html` — **Bitcoin Breaks Historic Highs: Institutional Adoption & The 2026 Macro Supercycle**
   - `blogs/2-central-banks-pivot.html` — **Global Central Banks Pivot: Interest Rates, Bond Yields & Portfolios**
   - `blogs/3-defi-3-revolution-rwa.html` — **The DeFi 3.0 Revolution: Liquid Restaking, Real World Assets (RWA) & Yields**
   - `blogs/4-ai-fintech-trading.html` — **AI in Fintech & Quantitative Trading: Wall Street Disruption**
   - `blogs/5-layer-2-scaling-race.html` — **Ethereum, Solana & Layer-2 Superchains: The Great Scaling Race**
   - `blogs/6-inflation-hedges-assets.html` — **Inflation Hedges in Modern Portfolios: Gold vs. Real Estate vs. Digital Scarcity**
   - `blogs/7-crypto-regulations-etfs.html` — **Global Crypto Regulation & ETFs: Unlocking Trillions in Capital**
   - `blogs/8-neobanks-crossborder-payments.html` — **Neobanks & Instant Cross-Border Rails: Death of SWIFT Latency**
   - `blogs/9-green-crypto-mining.html` — **Green Mining & The Energy Transition: Clean Power PoW**

3. **Interactive Article Features**:
   - **Top Dynamic Reading Progress Bar**: Real-time progress bar tracking scroll depth.
   - **Interactive Like Button**: Persistent like counters saving state in browser `localStorage`.
   - **Social Share / Copy URL**: Native share or automatic clipboard URL copy with toast notification.
   - **Live Commenting System**: Allows readers to post immediate comments with live DOM insertion.
   - **Table of Contents**: Jump navigation to any article subsection.
   - **Related Articles Recommendations**: Seamless cross-navigation between all posts.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Simply double click on [index.html](file:///c:/Users/Dolar/Downloads/adstra%20web%20one/index.html) in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
Run in PowerShell / Terminal:
```bash
npx serve . -l 3000
```
Then navigate to: `http://localhost:3000`
